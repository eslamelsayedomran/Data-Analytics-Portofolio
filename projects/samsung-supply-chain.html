The business question

How can operational teams view commercial performance alongside inventory availability, supplier reliability and shipping outcomes—and identify where deeper investigation is needed?

Analytical scope

  • Revenue, profit, sales channels and customer contribution
  • Stock levels, safety stock, reorder points and defective units
  • Supplier lead time, order volume and quality scores
  • Shipment status, delivery delays and carrier-level performance

What this project demonstrates

  • Cross-functional Power BI report design
  • Fact and dimension modeling with DAX measures
  • Executive-level KPIs with supporting operational drill-downs
  • Consistent visual navigation across analytical domains

Selected observations

Commercial performance

The Customer view displays 176.95M in total revenue, 48.56M in profit and a 27.44% profit margin. It also breaks down revenue by channel and customer.

Delivery execution

The Shipment view displays 8K shipments, a 75.29% delivered share and 573 delays. Carrier and delay-reason breakdowns help prioritize further investigation.

Inventory monitoring

The Inventory view compares stock, safety stock and reorder points by product, and tracks defective units over time. Quantity-based turnover is displayed as 28.1, with 12.99 inventory days.

Supplier oversight

The Supplier view compares lead times across countries and suppliers, alongside order quantities and quality scores.

Interpretation note: Figures above are transcribed from the provided dashboard screenshots. They describe this demonstration report, not verified operational results for Samsung. Turnover and inventory-day figures should be interpreted with their period and stock-snapshot assumptions.

From dashboard to action

01 / Identify

Locate the products, suppliers or carriers that stand out against baseline performance.

02 / Validate

Check calculation definitions, date coverage and data grain before treating patterns as operational problems.

03 / Prioritize

Use the validated results to focus stock planning, supplier reviews and shipment-delay investigation.
