Evidence from the supplied dataset

Length of stay and cost

Records with stays of 0–2 days have a mean cost of about $18,053, compared with $35,101 for stays of 6–10 days. This is an association, not evidence that shorter stays cause savings.

Patient severity matters

The 79 records classified as Extreme severity average approximately $63,485, compared with $20,376 across Minor severity records. Raw hospital rankings may therefore be misleading.

Decision framework

1. Compare fairly

Benchmark facilities within similar severity and patient groups, with minimum sample sizes.

2. Investigate outliers

Flag hospitals with high adjusted cost and prolonged length of stay for clinical and operational review.

3. Validate interventions

Evaluate discharge planning, care coordination and resource use before estimating financial savings.
