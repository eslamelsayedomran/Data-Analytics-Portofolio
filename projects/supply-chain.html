The business question

How can an operations team monitor sales performance, purchasing activity, stock distribution and delivery outcomes in one coherent reporting environment, and identify areas that merit closer investigation?

What the dashboard surfaces

Commercial & purchasing activity

The sales view displays approximately 8,000 orders, 30 customers and sales by country and customer. Procurement reporting tracks vendor spending and purchase order status.

Inventory & fulfillment

Inventory reporting shows material groups, stock distribution and warehouse-level balances. Logistics reporting includes deliveries, average delivery days and delivery status.

Priority for validation: The on-time delivery indicator is 33.66% in the supplied dashboard. Before drawing operational conclusions, its DAX definition, status logic and source records should be checked. Likewise, revenue, procurement and inventory values are different measures and should not be subtracted to infer profitability.

Analysis approach

The case study uses structured supply chain tables and a Power BI report to present operational KPIs, trends, category breakdowns and detailed records. It is a portfolio analytics exercise, not a claim of access to a live enterprise SAP deployment.

Potential next steps

  • Validate order-to-delivery relationships and the on-time delivery calculation.
  • Investigate delivery delays by warehouse, geography and period.
  • Examine stock availability and turnover alongside purchasing patterns.
  • Document the data model and key measures for technical review.
