HR Analytics

Exploring the recruitment pipeline, hiring outcomes and vacancy management through two complementary Power BI dashboards.

The business question

How can an HR team monitor hiring activity, identify recruitment bottlenecks and prioritize vacancies using a clear set of operational metrics?

This portfolio case study showcases two report views: Hiring and Vacancies. The figures below are shown as displayed in their respective dashboards; they should not be combined without validating their underlying populations and definitions.

266Hires · Hiring view

Dashboard walkthrough

01 / Hiring Dashboard

Application status, hiring by department, recruitment sources, experience mix, interview scores and offered salaries.

02 / Vacancy Dashboard

Vacancy priorities, open positions, fill time, cost per hire, requisition types and department-level hiring demand.

Questions supported by the report

  • Which departments account for the most hires and vacancies?
  • How are applications distributed across recruiting channels?
  • What do the vacancy priority and requisition mix look like?
  • Where might recruitment cycle times warrant investigation?

Analysis approach

Power BI reporting with KPI cards, categorical comparisons, distribution charts, recruitment funnel views and interactive department, location, status and source filters.

The accompanying project data includes cleaned HR tables. Metric definitions and table relationships should be verified before presenting cross-page conclusions.

Interpretation note. The Vacancy view includes a hiring funnel showing approximately 129K applicants and 2K hires, while the Hiring view displays 1,050 applications and 266 hires. These may represent different populations or calculations; this case study does not treat them as directly comparable.
