Approach

Analytics grounded in business context.

My work focuses on connecting business questions with reliable metrics and clear visual stories. Each case study highlights a different operational challenge — from inventory planning and shipment performance to recruitment and healthcare cost variation.

Rather than treating dashboards as the final deliverable, I aim to make them useful starting points for investigation, prioritization and action.
